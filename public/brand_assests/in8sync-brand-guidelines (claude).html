05 · Visual Motif

The Ring System

The orbital rings surrounding the "8" are the heart of In8Sync's visual language. They represent the connections, integrations, and orbiting data flows between business systems. This motif can be abstracted and used across marketing materials, backgrounds, and UI decoration.

Primary Icon Mark
The logo icon in isolation — use for favicons, app icons, and small-format applications.
Orbital Background
Concentric orbiting rings as a decorative background element — used in hero sections and slides.
Connection Nodes
Abstracted rings as data-node diagrams — used in integration diagrams and technical illustrations.
Ring Color Meaning: Teal rings (#5BB8D4) represent active connections and primary data flows. Gray rings (#9EA3AD) indicate supporting or secondary integrations. Black rings represent core system nodes.
06 · Brand Voice

How We Communicate

In8Sync speaks with clarity and confidence. We are experts in our field — but we don't talk down to our clients. Our tone is direct, technically credible, and results-focused.

Authoritative, Not Arrogant
We demonstrate expertise through precision and specificity — not jargon or bravado. We explain complex concepts clearly.
"Real-time bi-directional data sync — no middleware, no delays."
Practical, Not Abstract
We speak in outcomes and business results. Every feature statement connects back to what it means for operations.
"Eliminate manual data entry. Free your team for work that scales."
Partner, Not Vendor
We position ourselves as long-term partners in a client's NetSuite journey — not a one-time software sale.
"Dedicated ongoing support and optimization — we grow with you."
Confident, Not Pushy
Our CTAs invite rather than pressure. We let the strength of the product and our expertise speak for itself.
"Schedule a demo and see it working in your environment."

Tone Attributes

Expert Clear Results-focused Partner-minded Confident Salesy Jargon-heavy Casual Boastful

Writing Principles

Lead with outcomes

Start with what the client gains — then explain how. "Reduce fulfillment errors by 80%" before "via automated 3PL sync."

Be specific

Avoid "powerful" and "robust." Use specific capabilities: "API, EDI, and file-based connectivity."

Short sentences win

B2B buyers scan. Break complex ideas into clear, punchy sentences. One idea per sentence.

NetSuite is the hero

Frame In8Sync as the force that unlocks NetSuite's potential — not a replacement for it.

08 · Usage Rules

Logo Do's & Don'ts

Consistent logo application is critical to maintaining brand recognition. Follow these rules across all touchpoints.

Do
  • Use the logo on approved backgrounds only (white, navy, brand blue, light gray)
  • Maintain clear space equal to 1× cap-height on all sides
  • Use the full lockup (wordmark + icon) in most contexts
  • Use the original logo files provided in the brand kit
  • Use the icon mark alone for favicons and small-format use
  • Maintain aspect ratio when scaling the logo
  • Request new logo files from the brand team for special placements
Don't
  • Stretch, skew, or distort the logo in any direction
  • Change any element's color individually
  • Place the logo on busy photographic backgrounds without contrast
  • Apply drop shadows, glows, or other visual effects
  • Redraw or recreate the logo in any tool
  • Use the logo at sizes smaller than 120px (digital) or 28mm (print)
  • Rearrange or separate the "In8" from "Sync"

Color Don'ts
